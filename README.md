# LUMIÈRE BEAUTY STUDIO & WELLNESS SANCTUARY

A high-converting, responsive luxury landing page designed for an upscale beauty parlour and aesthetic wellness sanctuary.

Built around **quiet luxury**, warm editorial elegance, and serene wellness aesthetics.

---

## ✦ Visual & Brand Identity

- **Theme**: Quiet luxury, warm editorial elegance, and serene wellness sanctuary.
- **Color Palette**:
  - **Primary Background**: Warm Alabaster / Soft Cream (`#FAF7F2`)
  - **Secondary Surface**: Soft Blush / Champagne (`#F3EAE3`)
  - **Primary Text**: Deep Espresso / Charcoal (`#2C2420`)
  - **Accent Color**: Muted Rose Gold / Terracotta (`#C58F79`) for CTAs, badges, and key highlights
  - **Accent Gold**: (`#D4AF37`)
- **Typography**:
  - **Headings**: Editorial Serif `Playfair Display` & `Cormorant Garamond` (italics & delicate serifs)
  - **Body Text**: Clean Sans-Serif `Plus Jakarta Sans`
  - **Pricing & Timing Tags**: Monospace `Space Mono`
- **UI Details**:
  - Soft rounded corners (`16px–24px`)
  - Subtle glassmorphic sticky header (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`)
  - Micro-hover states on cards with gentle elevation and image scale
  - Thumb-friendly bottom sticky "Book Now" bar on mobile viewports

## ✦ Page Structure & Key Sections

1. **Sticky Glassmorphic Header**:
   - Minimalist logo with sub-line `BEAUTY STUDIO & SANCTUARY`.
   - Smooth navigation links: *Services*, *Bridal & Packages*, *Lookbook*, *Reviews*, *Location*.
   - Terracotta primary CTA button: `"Book Appointment"`.
   - Full mobile navigation drawer with hamburger toggle.

2. **Hero Section (Above the Fold)**:
   - Benefit-driven headline: *"Reveal Your Natural Radiance in Complete Tranquility."*
   - Editorial subheadline highlighting personalized treatments, certified master estheticians, and organic botanical products.
   - Dual CTAs: Primary solid button (`"Book Your Session"`) and secondary ghost button (`"Explore Menu"`).
   - Trust Bar: *"4.9★ Google Rating (540+ Happy Clients) • Certified Master Estheticians • 100% Organic & Cruelty-Free"*.
   - Floating award badge on photography visual: *"✦ Voted Best Luxury Sanctuary 2026 • Suite 100 VIP Salon"*.

3. **Interactive Service Menu & Pricing**:
   - 4 Category Tabs with instant filtering:
     - `Hair Styling & Spa` (French Balayage, Caviar Hair Spa, Couture Cut, Bio-Keratin)
     - `Skin & Facials` (Diamond Hydra-Facial, Buccal Sculpting, LED Phyto-Stem, Barrier Peel)
     - `Bridal & Party Makeup` (Couture Bridal Day, Preview Trial, Red Carpet Glam, Bridal Party)
     - `Nails & Lash Artistry` (Japanese Gel Art, Milk & Honey Pedicure, Cashmere Lashes, Keratin Lift)
   - Scannable cards showing service title, description, duration, starting price, and instant `"Book Service"` trigger.

4. **Signature Bridal & Glow Packages**:
   - 3-column tiered cards with feature checklists:
     - **The Essential Glow** ($180)
     - **The Royal Bridal Experience** ($480) — highlighted with **"MOST POPULAR"** badge, elevated rose-gold border, VIP suite reservation, and Veuve Clicquot champagne service.
     - **The Ultimate Spa Retreat** ($320)

5. **Before & After / Lookbook Gallery**:
   - Interactive Before/After image comparison sliders with draggable divider (`⇄`) for touch and mouse.
   - Visual showcase of dimensional hair transformations, glass skin hydra-facials, and editorial bridal artistry.

6. **Client Testimonials & Social Proof**:
   - Verified patron cards with 5-star ratings, authentic feedback, service tags, and locations (Camille d'Aubigny, Dr. Rachel Sterling, Vivienne Westwood-Sloane).
   - Highlighting over 500+ five-star Google reviews.

7. **Interactive Appointment Booking Engine (With WhatsApp Quick-Book)**:
   - Step 1: Select Treatment (1-click auto-select from service menu).
   - Step 2: Choose Preferred Specialist (Élise Laurent, Sophia Chen, Maya Al-Mansoor, or First Available) + Date & Time Slot (9:30 AM – 6:00 PM).
   - Step 3: Enter Guest Details with instant **WhatsApp Quick-Book** generation (`wa.me` deep-link) and SMS reservation confirmation modal with celebratory confetti.

8. **Footer & Local Studio Info**:
   - Studio address (428 Belmont Boulevard, Suite 100, West Hollywood).
   - Studio opening hours table (Mon–Sat 9AM–8PM, Sun 10AM–5PM).
   - 6-photo aesthetic Instagram feed grid preview (`@LumiereBeautyStudio`).
   - Quick contact links and zero-cancellation fee guarantee.

9. **Mobile-First UX Feature**:
   - **Thumb-friendly bottom sticky "Book Now" bar** pinned to the bottom of all mobile screens, showing studio rating and quick booking action.
